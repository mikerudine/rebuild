Mail & parcels

No open hromada-level parcel volumes exist. We use Nova Poshta network growth as a proxy: current point counts vs points whose Ref (UUIDv1) was created before 2021, joined to business-register growth 2021→2025.

National context

  • Nova Poshta (2025 results press): 522 mln parcels (+10% y/y); network +12248 points to 50003 (2195 branches, 10053 postomats) as of Jan 2026 — no regional breakdown published.
  • NCEC form 3-P postal: 9M 2025 total items +13% y/y (domestic +8%, international +126%); 2025 sector revenue 52.7 bn UAH (+27.4%) — national totals only.

Method

  • Point source: Nova Poshta public API getWarehouses (empty apiKey), snapshot of 54992 working locations (findings 2026-10-11).
  • ref_created from UUIDv1 Ref timestamp. Validation: 46966 refs before 2026-01-01 vs 50003 NP-reported (94%); before 2025-01-01: 35491 vs ~37755 (94%).
  • Hromada assignment via bounds.pkl: ~85% of points; unmapped mostly Kyiv city (not a hromada in bounds).
  • Joined to growth ranking: 1367 hromadas; full NP counts — np_hromada_counts.csv.

Caveats

  • Closed points drop out of the API — survivorship bias; pre-2021 bases are understated where the network shrank (especially frontline).
  • Pre-2021 branch Refs often cluster in 2014–2016 (NP system migration) — Ref date ≈ first appearance in today's system, not opening date.
  • Oblast-level "surviving growth" ratios mislead (Donetsk/Zaporizhzhia rank high because old points were lost) — use hromada level or western oblasts only.
  • Not parcel volumes; excludes Ukrposhta/Meest; OSM postal features are incomplete for NP.

Sources: Nova Poshta public API getWarehouses, Nova Poshta 2025 press results, NCEC form 3-P «Поштовий зв'язок», Derzhstat business register (growth join), findings 2026-10-11 ( hromada_growth_vs_np.csv, np_hromada_counts.csv).
