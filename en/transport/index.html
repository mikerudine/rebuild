Bus routes and carriers

Do new bus routes appear where business grows? Open route registers are rarely published nationwide, so this page uses Ivano-Frankivsk oblast, which publishes its full intra-oblast intercity and suburban route register every year, plus the national Ukrtransbezpeka carrier licence register.

Key points

  • The oblast network shrank: 587 → 523 routes (-10.9%), 2224 → 1911 daily round trips (-14.1%), 2019 → 2026. The main drop is in the 2023 register; bus counts barely moved (1147 → 1137).
  • A few hromadas grow: Bolekhiv (trips ×2), Korshiv, Starobohorodchany, Yezupil, Nyzhnii Verbizh. The largest relative losses are in Verkhnia, Voinyliv, Lanchyn and Bukachivtsi; the largest absolute loss is Tysmenytsia (−90 daily trips).
  • Nationally, bus carriers with a valid licence: 13595 (2018) → 15003 (2022) → 12229 (2023) → 13354 (2026). Legal entities grow steadily: 3360 → 4331; the 2023 drop is FOP licence cancellations.

Method

  • Ivano-Frankivsk oblast administration registers of intra-oblast intercity and suburban bus routes (18 snapshots 2018–2026) in the Ministry of Infrastructure form: name, length, timetable with every stop, assigned and reserve buses, carrier, contract.
  • Trips: the “daily round trips” field, or the number of timetable blocks where it is empty (87% agreement where both exist).
  • Stops matched to KATOTTG within the oblast (765 of 946 names, 92% of stop occurrences; ambiguous names resolved by the rayon of the route’s other stops). A route counts for every hromada where it stops.
  • Licences: quarterly Ukrtransbezpeka register snapshots 2018–2022 (data.gov.ua) and the full current register from the open API (94592 records, 2026-10-19): activity start date and cancellation date. A carrier is active in a year if it held a valid bus licence at year end.

Caveats

  • The register lists contracts, not trips actually run: a route can exist on paper without service.
  • Registers are updated irregularly (2023 = 2024 for suburban, 2025 = 2026), so changes cluster in particular years.
  • Licences carry no vehicle or seat counts — only carrier, activity type and dates.
  • The open business register (ЄДР) no longer includes addresses (legal entities) or tax IDs (FOPs), so a carrier’s hromada comes only from addresses in the registers themselves.
  • Interregional routes (Ukrtransbezpeka register) and city routes are not included.

Sources: Ivano-Frankivsk intra-oblast route register, Ukrtransbezpeka route register (IF 2018), Ukrtransbezpeka licence register (2018–2022 snapshots), open licence API, KATOTTG. CSV: transport_if_hromada_year.csv, transport_if_hromada_growth.csv, transport_carriers_ukraine_year.csv.
