Shabo

Village in Bilhorod-Dnistrovskyi rayon, Odesa oblast. Settlement KATOTTG from SDMX research: UA51040310010035609.

Economy and tourism

  • SHABO company and the Wine Culture Centre (opened 2009 per SHABO and Wikipedia).
  • Ekonomichna Pravda describes the company as town-forming and supporting village upkeep.
  • 60–70 thousand visitors per year — attributed to the company in Ekonomichna Pravda (December 2023); media quote, not Derzhstat.

Data on this site

Oblast-level wine/grape SDMX flows (e.g. DF_ARRIVAL_GRAPES_WINE_MATERIALS) do not break out Shabo village.
