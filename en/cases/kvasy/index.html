Українська

Kvasy

Village in Yasinia territorial hromada (Rakhiv rayon, Zakarpattia oblast). Settlement KATOTTG from SDMX research: UA21060070020094987; hromada — UA21060070000049340.

Context

  • Mineral water (burkut), spa tourism, Hirska Tysa sanatorium.
  • Youth NGO projects: station lighting, tourist information centre (per linked media).

Recognition (no unsourced population figures)

  • Finalist of «Incredible Villages of Ukraine 2022» (per linked coverage).
  • Nomination for UNWTO Best Tourism Villages 2024 (per tourism press — see sources).

Derzhstat SDMX has no population counts for individual villages or hromadas. See the Data page for hromada-level business-register indicators for Yasinia hromada, which includes Kvasy.
