Data

Overview of Derzhstat SDMX datasets with verified KATOTTG granularity. For most indicators the lowest level is hromada or rayon, not individual villages.

Open hromada growth rankings (business register, composite signals) on Kaggle: rebuild-ukraine-hromada-growth (hromada_growth_ranked.csv).

SDMX API base: https://stat.gov.ua/sdmx/workspaces/default:integration/registry/sdmx/2.1. The REGION dimension uses KATOTTG (31,753 codes). No dataflow exposes individual villages; the business register reaches hromada level (1,633 codes).
