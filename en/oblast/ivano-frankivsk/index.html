      Method and data freshness

      • Tiles: value, change against the baseline period (shown under the number) and the same change for Ukraine. Green if the oblast grew more than the country.
      • Successes / problems are rule-based: any indicator where the oblast beats the country goes to successes, any where it lags goes to problems; plus the top-3 and bottom-3 hromadas by legal entities, hromadas losing legal entities, and hromadas with no major-chain store.
      • Nova Poshta "pre-2021" points are recovered from the UUIDv1 Ref date and miss closed points; the national figure is inflated by big-city postomats.
      • work.ua covers only the oblast centre; OSM under-counts early years (see the Retail page).
      • Stores: the primary tile is ATB corrected for OSM completeness (official 2026 list vs 2021 estimate). Raw OSM shows +87%, mostly because stores get mapped over time, so it is only a footnote. Official lists of five chains give the current count without history.
      • Transport: suburban and intercity route registries (Ukrtransbezpeka and the oblast registry, data.gov.ua), 2019 → latest snapshot 2026-03-16. No Ukraine-wide route data exists, so the national comparison is only for bus carriers (licences + EDR address). Trip-change rules only name hromadas with ≥10 daily trips in 2019; details on the Transport page.
      • Education: UCEQA open data, counting only current-year school graduates, so "schools" means schools with 11th-graders where at least one pupil sat the test, not the whole network. Up to 2021 the ZNO was compulsory for all graduates, while the NMT (from 2022) is taken only by university applicants, so the like-for-like comparison is 2023→2025; the 2016–2021 ZNO series is shown as context. Schools are matched to hromadas by settlement name (89–94% of graduates), and for 2025 also by EDEBO/EDRPOU codes. Hromada change needs a base of ≥20 graduates (≥50 in the rules).
      • Housing: EDESSB construction registry, residential objects commissioned in January–September. Open dumps exist only for 2021 and 2026, so there are two time points. "Amnesty" houses (legalising earlier builds) are counted separately and excluded from the tile. New houses may include tourist cottages (especially in mountain hromadas). Rules use hromadas with ≥20 houses in 2021. Square metres come from Derzhstat at oblast level (not available per hromada; from 2024 country/garden houses are included).
