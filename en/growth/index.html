Caveats

  • The register counts registered units, not activity; post-2022 relocation inflates receiving hromadas — the intended signal.
  • Entities registered at oblast/city level are not allocated to hromadas.
  • Series starts 2021; no hromada population in SDMX — no per-capita view.
  • Filter excludes hromadas with >1000 legal entities in 2021; no hromada type in KATOTTG.
