Methodology

Where numbers come from, how we use SDMX, and known gaps in open data.

Derzhstat SDMX

  • Dataflow catalog: https://stat.gov.ua/sdmx/workspaces/default:integration/registry/sdmx/2.1/dataflow/all/all/latest (125 flows as of 2026-10-11 checks).
  • Regional availability: /availableconstraint/<DF>/all/all/REGION.
  • Data: /data/<DF>/<key>?startPeriod=YYYY. Keys must not start with «.» — put indicator codes first.
  • c[REGION]= filters are ignored; CSV Accept often returns 404 — we use XML.

Chain stores (OSM) & HoReCa

  • Chain stores: Geofabrik OSM snapshots, brand matching, official locators for completeness, ATB corrected using press anchors. CSVs in public/data/.
  • Food service: Derzhstat KVED section I (oblast); OSM food amenities via ohsome Jan-1 snapshots.
  • Mail & parcels: Nova Poshta API getWarehouses (UUIDv1 Ref creation time); hromada bounds join; merged with business-register growth ranking. National figures from Nova Poshta 2025 press (522M parcels) and NCEC form 3-P. CSVs: hromada_growth_vs_np.csv, np_hromada_counts.csv (findings 2026-10-11).

Hromada rankings & work.ua

  • Kaggle: rebuild-ukraine-hromada-growth — legal-entity/FOP growth 2021→2025, zones, composite (Derzhstat business register).
  • work.ua/stat — monthly median advertised salaries and vacancy counts by city (public/data/workua_city_monthly.csv).

Other sources (cases)

  • Media, hromada sites, SHABO materials — linked on case pages.
  • api.openbudget.gov.ua — expected hromada budget API; not verified from build infrastructure. No budget figures until verified.

Limitations

  • No village/hromada population in SDMX; DF_POPULATION_STRUCTURE is oblast-level.
  • War-related coverage gaps from 2022 onward in official metadata.
  • 2020 amalgamation changed hromada boundaries; KATOTTG vs legacy KOATUU.
  • Every chart ships with JSON including source, fetched_at, and query URL.

Build pipeline

Before astro build, scripts/fetch-sdmx.ts pulls verified series into public/data/. GitHub Actions deploys static output to Pages with base: /rebuild/.
