# Product & status

## Selo (the game/app) — not public yet
A revival game: the player brings a declining Ukrainian place back to life, driven by real data.

**Start flow:** player first picks a **place type**, then a **specific real place** to "revive":
1. Abandoned village in the Carpathians.
2. Ghost town around a former factory / industrial centre.
3. Small town in the east that survived occupation.

Each type needs **5–7 real examples** (Asana task 1219563323171890, in progress — not yet researched).

Earlier design work (chart-only "Hromada Atlas" UI) is in `design/` — see `docs/design.md`.

## Public research site (this repo)
Shows data on hromada-level growth signals: business registrations, vacancies, retail chains, HoReCa, Nova Poshta network, case studies (Kvasy, Shabo). **Must not mention the app/game.** Ukrainian by default, `/en` mirror.
Business relocation after 2022 is treated as a **positive trend** (regional development), not a confounder.

## Key findings so far
- Business growth concentrated in the west (top: Polyanytsia, Zarichanska, Chahor; Yasinia #103).
- work.ua: largest 3-yr vacancy growth Uzhhorod (+40%).
- ATB (OSM-corrected): Rivne +112%, Lviv +88%, Kharkiv −13%.
- Food-service FOPs +48% nationally.
- Nova Poshta network growth correlates 0.26–0.35 with business growth.
